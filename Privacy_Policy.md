Privacy Policy for VitthalNama
Last updated: November 3, 2026

VitthalNama ("the App") provides Marathi devotional content, including
Haripath and aarti. This Privacy Policy explains how information is
handled when you use the App.

The App does not require you to create an account. We do not ask you to
provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email
address to use the App.  

If the App is updated to include features or third-party services that
collect information, this policy will be updated to describe that
collection.

The App may request device permissions only when they are needed for a
feature. The purpose of each permission will be explained in context.
You can manage or revoke permissions through your device settings.  

3. Third-Party Services  
The App may use third-party services for functions such as app
distribution, crash reporting, analytics, or advertising. Those services
may process limited technical information in accordance with their own
privacy policies.

Before publishing this policy, list every third-party SDK or service
actually included in the App (for example, analytics, crash reporting,

this section or make it more specific if no such services are used.

4. Data Sharing  
We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed
by third-party service providers only where those services are actually
integrated into the App and are needed to provide or maintain the App.

5. Data Retention and Deletion  
Because the App does not require an account, there is no account profile
to delete. Any information handled by third-party services is subject to
the retention and deletion practices of those providers.

If the App later introduces accounts or stores personal information,
this section will be updated with instructions for requesting deletion.

6. Children's Privacy  
The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed to
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely
